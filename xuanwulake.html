Xuanwu Lake

Image from Mary Ma, Xuanwu Lake
Written by Mary Ma

六朝的风，吹过南京玄武湖千年。湖面涟漪轻晃，把王朝兴衰、市井烟火，都揉进波光里。湖岸垂柳摇曳，与远处建筑相映，古意和烟火气奇妙交融。

它是历史的容器，也是市民生活的背景。建议多设历史导览，让游客读懂湖畔典故；定期维护湖域生态，让水更清、景更活。

愿这汪湖水，继续串起过去与当下，成为南京人心里，始终温热的文化符号，附件为玄武湖实拍图，盼它永葆生机，讲未完的故事。

The winds of the Six Dynasties have blown across Nanjing's Xuanwu Lake for thousands of years. Ripples gently ripple the surface, capturing the rise and fall of dynasties and the bustling city life. Willows sway along the lakeshore, echoing with distant buildings in a wondrous blend of antiquity and the hustle and bustle of life.

It serves as a vessel of history and a backdrop for local life. We suggest offering more historical tours to help visitors understand the lake's history, and regular maintenance of the lake's ecology to enhance its clarity and vitality.

May this lake continue to connect the past and present, becoming a cultural symbol that remains a warm memory in the hearts of Nanjing residents. Attached are photos of Xuanwu Lake. May it remain vibrant and tell its unfinished story.

Written by Cao Zhuo

玄武湖——坐落于江苏南京，是江南的皇家园林，是金陵明珠。

印象最深是玄武湖的蓝调时刻，幽暗的天空浸染整个南京城，但玄武湖内映射点点星火使忧郁中带着柔和的温暖。就像无助的孩子在黑暗中躲入母亲的怀抱，母亲为孩子抓萤火虫消解情绪一般让人暖心。是一个无论晴天雨天雪天白天夜里都让人心旷神怡的地方，并且我个人认为玄武湖目前仍然是自然之景大于商业化的的地方。

保护建议：

1️⃣可以通过当代年轻人对于一二次元的热爱与玄武湖的景色相结合，让更多的人关注并响应环境保护

2️⃣定点设立服务亭与服务人员，当看见有破坏生态的事情及时制止

3️⃣可以划定核心保护区，将重点保护与保护划分，适当给予商业化获得资金从而更好投入对景点的保护

Xuanwu Lake, located in Nanjing, Jiangdu Province, accounts for a royal garden of Jiangnan, and it is the pearl of Jinlin (Nanking).

My most memorable moment for Xuanwu Lake is the blue hour. The gloomy sky drentches the entire city of Nanjing, but the fitful sparks reflected within the lake bring a gentle warmth to the melancholy. It's like a helpless child hiding in the darkness, her mother catching fireflies for her to soothe her sorrow, which forms a heartwarming scenario. Regardless of rain, shine, snow, day, and night, it's a place that brings peace and tranquility. Personally I believe that Xuanwu Lake still prioritizes natural beauty over commercialization.

Conservation Suggestions:

1️⃣ Combine the contemporary youth's love for animation characters with the beauty of Xuanwu Lake to raise the awareness of environmental protection for consistent beauty.

2️⃣ Establish designated service booths and staff to intervene in time when ecological damage is observed.

3️⃣ Designate core protected areas, segregate key areas for protection and conservation, and allow for appropriate commercialization to generate funding for better conservation.
