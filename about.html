Mission & Vision +

Heritmap is designed to tell stories about cultural heritage in China digitally in teenagers' eyes. Our mission is to advocate for preservation of cultural heritage and connect people with the beauty and significance of cultural sites through interactive tools like maps, photos, and stories.

Educational Value +

This website is not only a digital archive but also a study tool. By describing both the advantage and giving advices to the management of historical sites, Heritmap provides a comprehensive viewpoint about preservation and offers reviews with real-experiences.

How It’s Built +

This site is coded in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript under partial help from artificial intellegence*. Designed and managed by Jerry Sun, it uses interactive mapping and is hosted via Vercel.

What's The "BIG PLAN"? +

By the time of our graduation (May, 2026), the goal is to complete some detailed heritage pages as well as a whole-functioning web. Future plans include adding a user-submitted gallery (promote own experience), multilingual support (languages other than Chinese and English), and vacation planning features.
